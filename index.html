There's a gap in every insurance product between what the paperwork says and what customers actually need. Closing that gap is the daily work.

The work looks beyond what's written in a BRD to identify what's missing, aligns business and technology teams, and makes trade-offs when priorities compete — turning ambiguity into decisions, and decisions into shipped products.

Spotting the gaps early, turning them into decisions, and helping teams ship the right thing — that's the throughline.

Rebuilding How Claims Get Filed

In Planning

Problem

Customers registering a new Motor claim across web and mobile ran into friction in the existing Register Claims flow, enough to create drop-off and extra support load.

Decision

Redesign the Register Claims flow end-to-end, built around where customers were actually getting stuck when filing a new claim, rather than patching the existing process.

Ownership

  • Reviewed BRDs line by line to catch gaps between what's documented and what the business actually needs before development starts.
  • Validated every UI/UX design against Motor UX standards before handoff to developers.
  • Coordinated across IT, developers, solution architects, PMO, E-PMO, SMO, QA, UX, and Business Analysts to keep the build moving.
  • Resolved scope conflicts and missed requirements in real time rather than letting them stall delivery.

Outcome

The redesigned claim filing journey is currently in System Integration Testing (the last major checkpoint before it reaches live Motor Claims customers).

Making Claim Status Make Sense at a Glance

In Development

Problem

The existing claim tracking view lacked clarity and structure. Customers struggled to understand claim progress or what to do next, without changing the underlying claims process itself.

Decision

Redesign the tracking view for clarity across TPL, Comprehensive, and Total Loss without touching the underlying claims process, and simplify the invoice upload step.

Ownership

  • Mapped how TPL, Comprehensive, and Total Loss claims each carry different requirements and stages, with no single source of truth to start from.
  • Worked directly with developers to pull the APIs needed and with the claims department to fill the knowledge gaps.
  • Reviewed every design pass against the BRD to keep it aligned and easy to follow.
  • Simplified the invoice upload step so customers could add documents without confusion.

Outcome

A visually clearer tracking experience for both logged-in and guest users, with easier invoice upload.

Running Four Product Lines at Once

Live

Al Rajhi Takaful needed consistent, accountable delivery across TPL, Comprehensive, Smart, and SME on both web and mobile, instead of four disconnected efforts.

  • Engaged external vendors and consultants, negotiating scope and validating technical approaches.
  • Monitored live Motor services with the team, resolving production issues before they escalate.
  • Hunted for customer friction points and acquisition opportunities to keep pushing the products forward.

Outcome

Consistent, accountable delivery across TPL, Comprehensive, Smart, and SME, live on both web and mobile.

Teaching Bots to Do the Boring Parts

Live

Problem

Manual, repetitive tasks inside Motor operations were eating time that should have gone to higher-value work.

Decision

Automate the manual process exactly as it ran, building 2 end-to-end RPA workflows in UiPath, rather than redesigning the underlying workflow.

Ownership

  • Watched the manual process run step by step to understand it fully.
  • Translated the observed workflow into bot logic.
  • Built, tested, and deployed both bots into production end-to-end.

Outcome

Manual operational effort stays down across the automated workflows, freeing that time for work that needs a human.

Building Telematics Into the Motor Journey

In Planning

Problem

ART's new Telematics Platform tracks driving behavior through two mechanisms (a Telematics Device and a Mobile SDK), but raw trip and event data means nothing to a customer unless it's turned into a score, a tier, and a reason to keep driving safely.

Decision

Define customer-facing Global Score, Trip, and enrollment status views, paired with reward-tier logic (Premium, Gold, Silver) and cashback, rather than exposing raw trip data.

Ownership

  • Ran BRD quality reviews with the business analyst to close requirement gaps before development.
  • Defined the customer-facing score, trip, and enrollment status views.
  • Shaped the rewards logic sorting customers into tiers and calculating cashback.
  • Drafted compliance-focused guidance on SDK activation models and mapped the enrollment flow end-to-end.

Outcome

Requirements and enrollment flow are locked; the score, trip views, and cashback/rewards logic are in build.

Cutting a Confusing Form Down to Size

Live

Problem

The Motor policy request-service form buried customers under 15 loosely organized services spread across four sections with overlapping labels, confusing more than it helped.

Decision

Narrow 15 services down to 5 tied directly to the customer's policy, and rebuild the form around 5 straightforward fields instead of 4 overlapping sections.

Ownership

  • Audited all 15 existing services to find which were redundant or unclear.
  • Narrowed them to 5 core services tied directly to the customer's policy.
  • Rebuilt the form around the simplified structure.

Outcome

A 67% reduction in service options. Customers now get a shorter, policy-relevant form instead of a maze of overlapping options.

Giving the Sales Link a Proper Welcome

Soon

Problem

The journey customers landed on after clicking a sales-sent quotation link had no welcoming entry point and no flexibility to move between viewing the quote and paying, leading to confusion and drop-off.

Decision

Add a dedicated welcome screen with a clear choice between viewing the quote or going straight to payment, aligned to updated branding standards.

Ownership

  • Defined the problem: a flow that didn't guide users in or reflect modern UX standards.
  • Designed the welcome screen and quote-or-pay options.
  • Aligned the UI with updated design and branding standards.

Outcome

A guided entry point with clear next steps, replacing the old unguided flow, now in final steps before release.

Getting Motor TPL Down to One Fast Checkout

In Development

Problem

The standard Motor TPL sales journey asked customers for information ART already had and offered no fast payment option, slowing down a purchase that should be quick.

Decision

Pull existing customer data instead of re-asking for it, and add Sequence Number vehicle lookup and Apple Pay to cut steps out of the journey.

Ownership

  • Scoped the shortened journey to avoid re-asking customers for data ART already has.
  • Defined the Sequence Number vehicle lookup to skip manual entry.
  • Added Apple Pay to the requirements alongside ART's existing payment methods.

Outcome

A faster, lower-friction TPL purchase on both web and mobile, past requirements and now being built.

Bringing Motor Insurance Into Tawakkalna

In Development

Problem

ART sold Motor Insurance only through its own digital channels and aggregators, missing a trusted, government-backed channel with verified customer data already on file.

Decision

Use Tawakkalna's verified data instead of re-collecting it, avoiding duplicate account creation and opening a new, trusted distribution channel.

Ownership

  • Defined how ART would use Tawakkalna's verified data instead of re-collecting it.
  • Scoped the purchase journey to avoid duplicate account creation.
  • Built the case for a new, trusted distribution channel.

Outcome

A direct-to-Tawakkalna Motor Insurance journey, now moving from requirements into build.

Keeping the WhatsApp Sales Journey Running

Live

Problem

The Motor insurance sales journey inside WhatsApp needed to stay reliable and current in production, with issues surfacing and requirements shifting after launch rather than settling once and for all.

Decision

Treat the WhatsApp journey as a live, continuously managed channel: fix production issues as they surface and route enhancements through vendor Change Requests instead of leaving the journey static after go-live.

Ownership

  • Resolved live production issues affecting the WhatsApp sales journey.
  • Drove iterative enhancements to improve the journey over time.
  • Negotiated Change Requests directly with the vendor to scope and prioritize fixes.

Outcome

A stable, continuously improving Motor sales channel on WhatsApp.
